About KBG

Barbering, done properly

KBG Barbershop is a professional barbershop built around one idea: every client should leave the chair looking sharper than when they sat down. From classic tapers to modern skin fades and detailed beard work, our barbers combine steady hands with an eye for what actually suits you — not just what's trending.

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead means less waiting and more time in the chair with your barber.
